Test trading rules on history before you trust them

A browser-based research workbench for Taiwan-style equity strategies. Run single-asset and portfolio backtests, map parameter sensitivity, test out-of-sample splits, and export results. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Simulated data. All prices here come from a random-walk generator with set drift and volatility (SIM-01 to SIM-08). They are not real market data and do not reflect any real company.

What you can do

  • Single-asset backtest. SMA crossover, Donchian channel or momentum rules, with a trade blotter and sortable results.
  • Risk exits. Stop loss, take profit and trailing stop, each set independently.
  • Out-of-sample split. Choose a split point to see in-sample and out-of-sample results side by side.
  • Zoom and drawdown. Drag across any chart to zoom, double-click to reset, and read the drawdown chart beneath.
  • Portfolio. Choose any mix of eight simulated stocks, equal or inverse-volatility weights, rebalance frequency and a per-asset cap, with cash for the remainder.
  • Parameter map. A heat map of return or Sharpe across fast and slow windows. Click a cell to open it in the workbench.
  • Costs you control. Commission, sell tax and slippage are editable, and can be switched off to see their effect.
  • Downloads. Equity and trade CSV files, and a standalone HTML report of the current result.

What this is not

  • Not investment advice, and not a recommendation of any stock or strategy.
  • No live prices, no broker connection, and no orders are placed.
  • Past backtest results do not predict future performance.

Methods and assumptions

How trades are timed

  • Signals use closing prices. A signal at the close of day t opens or closes a position on day t+1. There is no look-ahead.
  • Parameters are fixed inside each run. The out-of-sample split uses the same parameters on later data, so it tests stability, not optimisation.

Costs

  • Commission is charged on both buys and sells. Sell tax applies to sells. Slippage applies on both sides.
  • Defaults: commission 0.1425%, sell tax 0.3%, slippage 0.05% per side. You can change them on each page.
  • Trade returns in the blotter are approximate: they net out the entry and exit costs from the price move.

Risk exits

  • Stop loss exits when the close falls the set percentage below the entry price.
  • Take profit exits when the close rises the set percentage above the entry price.
  • Trailing stop exits when the close falls the set percentage below the highest close since entry.
  • After any risk exit the position stays flat until the signal turns off and on again.

Portfolio rules

  • Each symbol runs the same rule. Only symbols with an active signal are held.
  • Equal weight splits capital evenly among active symbols. Inverse volatility weights by the reciprocal of 60-day volatility.
  • Each weight is capped at the maximum you set. Any remainder is held as cash.
  • Rebalancing applies turnover costs. Between rebalances, weights drift with prices.

Metrics

  • CAGR uses 252 trading days per year. Sharpe and Sortino assume a zero risk-free rate.
  • Max drawdown is the largest fall from a previous peak in equity.
  • Profit factor is total gains of winning trades divided by total losses of losing trades.
  • Time in market is the share of days with an open position.

Limitations

  • Prices are simulated. Real markets have fat tails, regime changes, liquidity limits and corporate actions this model does not include.
  • No dividends, no borrowing, no shorting, no intraday execution and no market impact beyond the fixed slippage rate.
  • Results depend on the random data set. Use the new-data button to see how much results vary.
