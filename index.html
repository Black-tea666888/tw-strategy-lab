A research workbench for rules-based equity strategies

For instructors, analysts and quantitative researchers who need transparent, reproducible backtests. Every result shows its parameters, cost assumptions, random seed and out-of-sample split, and can be exported as a standalone report.

Current data: simulated. The workbench runs on a seeded random-walk generator (SIM-01 to SIM-08). It is not real market data. Each result is reproducible from its seed.

Open the strategy workbench →   Read the methods →

Built for

  • Instructors who teach systematic trading and need a transparent tool students can inspect.
  • Analysts who test a rule's sensitivity before deciding whether it deserves further work.
  • Researchers who need documented assumptions, out-of-sample splits and exportable records.

What you can do

  • Single-asset backtest. SMA crossover, Donchian channel and momentum rules, with a full trade blotter.
  • Risk exits. Stop loss, take profit and trailing stop, set independently and shown in every trade record.
  • Out-of-sample split. In-sample and out-of-sample metrics side by side, with the split marked on every chart.
  • Portfolio. Eight symbols, equal or inverse-volatility weights, rebalancing, a per-symbol cap and an equal-weight benchmark.
  • Parameter map. Return or Sharpe across fast and slow windows, to test whether a result depends on one narrow setting.
  • Costs you control. Commission, sell tax and slippage are editable, and can be switched off to isolate their effect.
  • Reproducible exports. Equity and trade CSV files, and an HTML report that records parameters, costs and the data disclosure.

Not included yet

  • Real market data. Connecting licensed Taiwan market data is the planned next step.
  • Live trading, brokerage connections or order routing. The workbench does not place orders.
  • Investment advice, stock recommendations or return forecasts.

Methods and assumptions

Every number on this site follows the rules below. If a rule is not listed here, the workbench does not apply it.

Data

  • All prices are simulated. Each dataset comes from a seed, so the same seed and parameters always reproduce the same result.
  • The seed is shown on every page and written into every HTML report.

How trades are timed

  • Signals use closing prices. A signal at the close of day t opens or closes a position on day t+1. There is no look-ahead.
  • Parameters are fixed inside each run. The out-of-sample split uses the same parameters on later data, so it tests stability, not optimisation.

Costs

  • Commission is charged on both buys and sells. Sell tax applies to sells. Slippage applies on both sides.
  • Defaults: commission 0.1425%, sell tax 0.3%, slippage 0.05% per side. You can change them on each page.
  • Trade returns in the blotter are approximate: they net out the entry and exit costs from the price move.

Risk exits

  • Stop loss exits when the close falls the set percentage below the entry price.
  • Take profit exits when the close rises the set percentage above the entry price.
  • Trailing stop exits when the close falls the set percentage below the highest close since entry.
  • After any risk exit the position stays flat until the signal turns off and on again.

Portfolio rules

  • Each symbol runs the same rule. Only symbols with an active signal are held.
  • Equal weight splits capital evenly among active symbols. Inverse volatility weights by the reciprocal of 60-day volatility.
  • Each weight is capped at the maximum you set. Any remainder is held as cash.
  • Rebalancing applies turnover costs. Between rebalances, weights drift with prices.

Metrics

  • CAGR uses 252 trading days per year. Sharpe and Sortino assume a zero risk-free rate.
  • Max drawdown is the largest fall from a previous peak in equity.
  • Profit factor is total gains of winning trades divided by total losses of losing trades.
  • Time in market is the share of days with an open position.

Limitations

  • Prices are simulated. Real markets have fat tails, regime changes, liquidity limits and corporate actions this model does not include.
  • No dividends, no borrowing, no shorting, no intraday execution and no market impact beyond the fixed slippage rate.
  • Results depend on the random data set. Use the new-data button to see how much results vary.
