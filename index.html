Check a strategy against history

A free, open-source backtesting and paper-trading tool for Taiwan stocks. This page lets you try the backtest workbench, strategy comparison and metric definitions. The full feature set runs on your own computer. The tool places no orders and holds no funds.

Metric definitions

MetricDefinition
Total returnFinal equity minus 1, net of costs.
CAGRTotal growth compounded per year, using 252 trading days per year.
Max drawdownThe largest fall of equity from a prior peak.
SharpeMean daily return over its standard deviation, times √252. Risk-free rate assumed zero.
SortinoLike Sharpe, but the denominator uses only downside volatility.
CalmarCAGR divided by the absolute max drawdown.
Profit factorTotal gains of winning trades divided by total losses of losing trades.
EntriesHow many times the strategy opened a position, including one still open at the end.
Buy & holdTotal return of buying on day one and holding to the end. Used as a benchmark.
