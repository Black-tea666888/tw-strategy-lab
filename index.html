指標說明Metric definitions

指標Metric定義Definition
總報酬Total return期末淨值減 1，已扣除成本。Final equity minus 1, net of costs.
年化報酬（CAGR）CAGR以 252 個交易日為一年，把總報酬換算成每年複利。Total growth compounded per year, using 252 trading days per year.
最大回撤Max drawdown淨值從歷史高點下跌的最大幅度。The largest fall of equity from a prior peak.
Sharpe每日報酬的平均值除以標準差，再乘上 √252。假設無風險利率為 0。Mean daily return over its standard deviation, times √252. Risk-free rate assumed zero.
Sortino類似 Sharpe，但分母只用下跌的波動。Like Sharpe, but the denominator uses only downside volatility.
Calmar年化報酬除以最大回撤的絕對值。CAGR divided by the absolute max drawdown.
獲利因子Profit factor獲利交易的總獲利除以虧損交易的總虧損。Total gains of winning trades divided by total losses of losing trades.
進場次數Entries策略建立部位的次數（含期末仍持有的部位）。How many times the strategy opened a position, including one still open at the end.
買進持有Buy & hold第一天買進並持有到期末的總報酬，作為基準。Total return of buying on day one and holding to the end. Used as a benchmark.
